Theory: Physical Beam Balance

Principle of Moments: A beam balance works on the principle of moments. When the beam is freely suspended and horizontal:

$$\text{Anti-clockwise Moment} = \text{Clockwise Moment} \iff m_1 g \cdot l_1 = m_2 g \cdot l_2$$

For equal-arm laboratory balances ($l_1 = l_2$):

$$m_1 = m_2$$

Physical Tilting & Pointer Behavior:

  • Left Pan Heavier ($m_1 > m_2$): The left pan sinks downwards. The downward-pointing needle tilts towards the RIGHT side of the ivory scale.
  • Right Pan Heavier ($m_2 > m_1$): The right pan sinks downwards. The downward-pointing needle tilts towards the LEFT side of the ivory scale.
  • Equilibrium ($m_1 \approx m_2$): The pointer oscillates symmetrically on either side of the zero division.
