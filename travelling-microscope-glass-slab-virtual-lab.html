🎯 Aim of the Experiment

To determine the refractive index of a glass slab using a travelling microscope by measuring its real thickness and apparent thickness.

📐 Working Formula & Vernier Constant
$$\mu = \frac{\text{Real Depth}}{\text{Apparent Depth}} = \frac{R_3 - R_1}{R_3 - R_2}$$

Least Count of Travelling Microscope:
$1\text{ Main Scale Division (MSD)} = 0.05\text{ cm} = 0.5\text{ mm}$
$50\text{ Vernier Scale Divisions (VSD)} = 49\text{ MSD}$
$$\text{Least Count (LC)} = 1\text{ MSD} - 1\text{ VSD} = \frac{1\text{ MSD}}{50} = \frac{0.05\text{ cm}}{50} = 0.001\text{ cm} = 0.01\text{ mm}$$

🔬 Why Apparent Depth is Less than Real Depth

When light rays originate from the ink mark on paper and travel through the denser glass slab into rarer air, they refract away from the normal. When produced backward, the rays appear to diverge from a virtual image located above the actual mark. The vertical apparent shift is given by: $$\Delta y = t\left(1 - \frac{1}{\mu}\right)$$
