Theory: Screw Gauge (Micrometer) & Least Count

Principle of Screw: The linear distance moved by the screw forward or backward during one full complete rotation of the thimble is called its Pitch.

$$\text{Least Count (LC)} = \frac{\text{Pitch}}{\text{Total Number of Circular Scale Divisions (CSD)}}$$

Zero Error and Correction: When the stud $A$ and spindle $B$ are in clean contact without any object:

  • Nil Error: Zero of the circular scale aligns exactly with the horizontal reference baseline of the main scale.
  • Positive Zero Error ($+e$): The circular zero lies below the baseline ($e = +n \times \text{LC}$).
  • Negative Zero Error ($-e$): The circular zero lies above the baseline ($e = -(N - n) \times \text{LC}$).
  • True Measurement: $\text{True Reading} = \text{Observed Reading} - (\pm e)$.

Volume of Wire & Area of Sheet:

$$\text{Wire Area: } A = \pi \left(\frac{D}{2}\right)^2 \quad\quad \text{Wire Volume: } V = A \cdot L$$
