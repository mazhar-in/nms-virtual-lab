Mathematical Statement: Current $I$ is directly proportional to potential difference $V$:
$V = I R \iff \frac{V}{I} = \text{Constant}$

Theory: Verification of Ohm's Law

Statement: The electric current $I$ passing through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference $V$ across its ends, provided temperature and other physical conditions remain constant.

$$V \propto I \implies V = I R$$

Verification: The ratio $V / I$ remains constant for all current levels, and the $V\text{–}I$ graph is a straight line passing through the origin. The slope of this straight line gives the resistance $R$ of the wire:

$$\text{Slope} = \frac{\Delta V}{\Delta I} = R$$
